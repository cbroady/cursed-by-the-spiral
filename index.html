Cursed by the Spiral

The Life and Times of a Snail Enthusiast

Hi there!

I’m Craig Broady, a Boston-based snail enthusiast, mechanical engineer, and cat parent. Originally this blog was started to document my sabbatical in spring of 2019, when I spent seven weeks traveling the west coast. Now it’s an occasional collection of my thoughts.

While my posts can generally be read in any order, I recommend starting at the beginning of my travels here.

This is a frozen archive of the blog as it stood in 2021. Comments and subscriptions have been retired.
